AI Customer Support Company Policy

Refund Policy
-------------
1. Customers can request a refund within 7 days of delivery.
2. Damaged or defective products are eligible for a full refund.
3. Refunds are processed within 5 business days after approval.

Shipping Policy
---------------
1. Shipping delays longer than 10 days are eligible for compensation.
2. Lost shipments should be escalated to the Logistics Team.

Billing Policy
--------------
1. Billing disputes require invoice verification.
2. Duplicate payments are eligible for a refund after verification.

Technical Support Policy
------------------------
1. Critical technical issues should be resolved within 12 hours.
2. Standard technical issues should be resolved within 24 hours.

General Policy
--------------
1. If the policy does not cover a case, escalate it to Customer Support.
2. Never promise anything outside these policies.
